Dragons Layer

Wizards of the Coast is launching 'D&D Universes Beyond' with a Star Wars crossover (Season of Rebellion) and Dark Sun return in 2027. The company introduces 'D&D Icons' for legendary creators and shifts to outside studios for official content. D&D Beyond updates include new drops, maps, and the 'Dungeon Masters' actual play series set in Ravenloft.

  • D&D Universes Beyond and Star Wars Crossover: Wizards of the Coast officially confirmed the 'D&D Universes Beyond' product line featuring crossovers with major IPs. A specific D&D Star Wars crossover, titled 'Season of Rebellion,' is scheduled for release in 2027. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons Fanatics | The Latest D&D News & Reviews)
  • Dark Sun Return and 18+ Rating: The Dark Sun setting is returning with five new D&D 5.5E products, all carrying an official 18+ rating due to their brutal and bloody nature. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons Fanatics | The Latest D&D News & Reviews)
  • New Publishing Lines: Icons and Outside Studios: Wizards announced 'D&D Icons,' a line pairing legendary creators with current designers, and revealed a strategic shift to use outside studios for producing official content in established settings. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons Fanatics | The Latest D&D News & Reviews)
  • Dungeon Masters Actual Play Series: An official actual play series titled 'Dungeon Masters' premiered on YouTube, featuring a campaign set in Ravenloft ('The Horrors Within') with an original score by David Arkenstone. (Fonte: Wizards of the Coast Announces Dungeon Masters)
  • D&D Beyond Updates and Roadmap: D&D Beyond released updates including shared dice rolls, map features, and specific drops like 'Sewers and Treasures' and 'Para-elementals.' The 2026 roadmap highlights new monsters, spells, and the return of obscure species. (Fonte: D&D Beyond | Play Your Way With the Official D&D Toolset)
  • Greyhawk Campaign: Crown of the Witch Queen: A new official campaign set in the Greyhawk world, titled 'Crown of the Witch Queen,' was revealed and is scheduled for release in late 2027. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons Fanatics | The Latest D&D News & Reviews)
