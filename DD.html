Dragons Layer

Dungeons & Dragons has officially adopted the name '5.5 Edition' for its latest ruleset, marking a strategic shift toward a digital-first model with significant updates to D&D Beyond scheduled for 2026-2027. Key upcoming content includes the 'Ravenloft: The Horrors Within' campaign, a 'World of Warcraft' collaboration, and the return of Dark Sun in 2027, alongside new physical products like the Curse of Strahd Legendary Edition.

  • [Official Naming of 5.5 Edition]: Hasbro and Wizards of the Coast have officially named the latest ruleset 'Dungeons and Dragons 5.5 Edition', replacing previous ambiguous titles like 'D&D 2024'. This change reflects a broader corporate strategy to position D&D as a digital-first IP company, with plans to rebuild D&D Beyond for a major shakeup in 2027. (Fonte: Hasbro Just Changed Dungeons & Dragons Forever)
  • [D&D Beyond Digital Roadmap and Updates]: D&D Beyond is implementing a 'Seasonal' update model, starting with the 'Season of Horror'. Recent updates include new maps tools (fullscreen mode, fog of war adjustments), the release of 'Grim Hollow: Player Pack', and the addition of books like 'Lairs of Etharis'. The platform is also preparing for a 2027 digital transition. (Fonte: Changelog - D&D Beyond)
  • [Upcoming Content Releases and Collaborations]: Major upcoming releases include 'Ravenloft: The Horrors Within' launching in Summer 2026, a 'World of Warcraft' D&D expansion, and the return of the 'Dark Sun' setting in 2027. Additionally, new physical products such as the 'Curse of Strahd Legendary Edition' box set and 'Mind Flayer Dice' are scheduled for release. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons State of the Game | Seasons and Design)
  • [Community and Organized Play Events]: The D&D community is preparing for Gen Con 2026, featuring the 'D&D Tower' and actual play livestreams. The 'D&D Fan Expo: London' is scheduled for September 4-5, 2027. Organized play initiatives include 'D&D Encounters' returning with Ravenloft themes and store-based rewards. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons | Official D&D Game, News, and Resources)
