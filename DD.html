Dragons Layer

Wizards of the Coast is executing a major strategic shift toward digital-first operations and cross-IP crossovers under Hasbro's direct oversight. Key announcements include the 'Universes Beyond' line featuring Star Wars (2027) and World of Warcraft (Nov 2026), alongside a new 'Icons' publishing line for legendary creators. The company is also launching 'Dungeon Masters,' an official actual-play series, and refocusing D&D Beyond as a central digital hub with significant updates planned for 2026-2027.

  • Universes Beyond: Star Wars and World of Warcraft Crossovers: Wizards of the Coast officially confirmed the 'D&D Universes Beyond' product line, starting with a World of Warcraft crossover releasing in November 2026. Additionally, an official D&D Star Wars crossover is scheduled for later in 2027 as part of the 'Season of Rebellion.' These initiatives mirror the success of Magic: The Gathering's crossovers. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons Fanatics | The Latest D&D News & Reviews)
  • Strategic Shift to Digital-First and Leadership Changes: Hasbro CEO Chris Cox has taken direct oversight of Wizards of the Coast following John Height's departure, confirming a 'digital first' future for D&D. This includes rebuilding D&D Beyond into a 'walled garden' by 2027, integrating AI, and moving players toward digital tools, despite previous setbacks with the Sigil VTT project. (Fonte: Hasbro Just Changed Dungeons & Dragons Forever)
  • Launch of 'Dungeon Masters' Actual Play Series and Ravenloft Content: Wizards announced 'Dungeon Masters,' an official actual-play series debuting April 22 on YouTube, featuring an all-star cast and unreleased content. The first campaign arc is set in Ravenloft, tying into the release of 'Ravenloft: The Horrors Within' on June 16, accompanied by a new DM screen and organized play updates. (Fonte: Wizards of the Coast Announces Dungeon Masters, an Official Dungeons & Dragons Actual Play Series)
  • New Publishing Lines: Icons and Dark Sun Return: The company introduced 'D&D Icons,' a publishing line pairing legendary creators with current designers, and announced the return of Dark Sun with five new 5.5E products rated 18+. Additionally, a new Greyhawk campaign titled 'Crown of the Witch Queen' is set for late 2027, utilizing outside studios for established settings. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons Fanatics | The Latest D&D News & Reviews)
