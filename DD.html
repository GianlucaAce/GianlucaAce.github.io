Dragons Layer

Wizards of the Coast is executing a major strategic shift toward digital-first play and external collaborations, confirmed by Hasbro's rebranding as a 'digital first play and IP company' with D&D Beyond being rebuilt for 2027. Key product announcements include the 'D&D Universes Beyond' line featuring a Star Wars crossover in 2027, the return of Dark Sun with an 18+ rating, and the 'Dungeon Masters' official actual play series debuting April 2026.

  • Strategic Shift to Digital-First and D&D Beyond Overhaul: Hasbro has officially designated Dungeons & Dragons as a 'digital first play and IP company,' with Chris Cox confirming that D&D Beyond is being refocused and rebuilt in 2026 for a major shakeup in 2027. This move aims to create a 'digital walled garden' following the collapse of the Sigil VTT project, emphasizing digital tools over physical play. (Fonte: Hasbro Just Changed Dungeons & Dragons Forever)
  • D&D Universes Beyond and Star Wars Crossover: Wizards of the Coast confirmed the 'D&D Universes Beyond' product line, launching crossovers with major IPs similar to Magic: The Gathering. A specific D&D Star Wars crossover was revealed at Gen Con for release in 2027 during the 'Season of Rebellion,' alongside a new Greyhawk campaign titled 'Crown of the Witch Queen.' (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons Fanatics | The Latest D&D News & Reviews)
  • Return of Dark Sun with 18+ Rating and New Publishing Lines: Dark Sun is returning to D&D 5.5E with five new products carrying an official 18+ rating due to brutal content. Additionally, Wizards announced 'D&D Icons,' a publishing line pairing legendary creators with current designers, and tapped outside studios to produce official content for established settings. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons Fanatics | The Latest D&D News & Reviews)
  • Launch of 'Dungeon Masters' Official Actual Play Series: Wizards of the Coast announced 'Dungeon Masters,' an official actual play series featuring an all-star cast, debuting April 22, 2026, on YouTube. The show will showcase unreleased D&D content and innovative visuals to appeal to both veterans and newcomers. (Fonte: Wizards of the Coast Announces Dungeon Masters, an Official Dungeons & Dragons Actual Play Series)
