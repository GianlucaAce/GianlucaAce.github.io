Dragons Layer

Wizards of the Coast is implementing a major strategic shift towards digital-first play and a new 'Seasons' content model, starting with 'Season of Rebellion' in late 2027. Key upcoming releases include an official D&D Star Wars crossover, the return of Dark Sun with an 18+ rating, and new Greyhawk campaigns, while D&D Beyond undergoes significant updates to support this ecosystem.

  • D&D Seasons Model and Strategic Shift: Wizards of the Coast announced a new 'Seasons' model for content delivery, beginning with 'Season of Rebellion' in late 2027. This shift emphasizes digital-first play, with D&D Beyond being rebuilt in 2026 for a 2027 launch as a central hub. The company is also tapping outside studios to produce official content for established settings under the new 'D&D Icons' publishing line. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons State of the Game | Seasons and Design)
  • Official Star Wars Crossover and Dark Sun Return: An official Dungeons & Dragons Star Wars crossover is confirmed for later in 2027. Additionally, the Dark Sun setting is returning with five new 5.5E products featuring an 18+ rating, described as 'brutal and bloody.' A new Greyhawk campaign titled 'Crown of the Witch Queen' is also scheduled for late 2027. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons Fanatics | The Latest D&D News & Reviews)
  • New Classes and Content Releases: The 'Magus' class, a mix of magic and martial abilities compatible with 5.5E, has been released on D&D Beyond via Sterling Vermin Adventure Co. Other notable releases include 'Dungeons! Illustrated Maps of Classic D&D Adventures' by Jason Bradley Thompson and the 'Ravenloft: The Horrors Within' expansion featuring new Darklords. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons Fanatics | The Latest D&D News & Reviews)
  • D&D Beyond Platform Updates and Roadmap: D&D Beyond is introducing a digital roadmap for 2026-2027, including new features like 'Polygonal Fog of War,' shared dice rolls on mobile, and the 'Thieves' Gallery' NPC pack. The platform also launched the D&D Community Advisory Group and updated its search function, while preparing for the forced migration to its new digital ecosystem. (Fonte: Changelog - - D&D Beyond)
