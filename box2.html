Call of Cthulhu encompasses both a tabletop RPG by Chaosium and a 2018 video game by Cyanide. The TTRPG uses the Basic Roleplaying (BRP) d100 system, set primarily in the 1920s, where players investigate cosmic horror while managing sanity mechanics. The video game adapts this into a third-person survival horror experience featuring investigator Pierce on Darkwater Island, utilizing Unreal Engine and focusing on narrative-driven mystery and psychological dread.

Key Findings

  • [Tabletop RPG Mechanics and Setting]: Call of Cthulhu is a horror TTRPG published by Chaosium that utilizes the Basic Roleplaying (BRP) system, specifically a d100 percentile dice mechanic. The game typically focuses on investigators in the 1920s (Classic Era), though other settings like the 1890s or 1980s are available. Key mechanics include skill checks for actions and a Sanity system where exposure to cosmic horrors causes mental degradation, potentially leading to madness or death. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu – Getting Started)
  • [Video Game Adaptation Details]: The video game 'Call of Cthulhu' was developed by Cyanide Studio and published by Focus Home Interactive, released on October 29, 2018. Built on the Unreal Engine, it is a third-person survival horror game where players control Private Investigator Pierce investigating the Hawkins family death on Darkwater Island in 1924. The gameplay involves solving mysteries, managing sanity levels that affect perception and reality, and confronting cultists and cosmic entities. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu - Tabletop Role-Playing Games (TTRPGs) - LibGuides at University of Missouri - St Louis)
  • [Literary Origins and Mythos]: The franchise is based on H.P. Lovecraft's 1928 novella 'The Call of Cthulhu,' which introduced the Cthulhu Mythos, the entity Cthulhu, and the city of R'lyeh. The story established the concept of ancient, slumbering gods and secret cults waiting for their return. Chaosium's RPG draws from this lore, emphasizing themes of cosmic indifference and the fragility of human sanity when confronted with the unknown. (Fonte: "The Call of Cthulhu" by H. P. Lovecraft)
  • [Starter Set and Accessibility]: Chaosium offers a 'Call of Cthulhu Starter Set' designed for new players, containing rules, five pre-generated investigators, dice, handouts, and four adventures ('Alone Against The Flames', 'Paper Chase', 'Edge of Darkness', 'Dead Man Stomp'). This set allows immediate play without prior knowledge of the system, serving as an entry point to the BRP engine and the broader Cthulhu Mythos universe. (Fonte: Chaosium Inc Call of Cthulhu Starter Set: Chaosium Inc., Mike Mason, Mike Mason: 9781568824635: Amazon.com: Books)
