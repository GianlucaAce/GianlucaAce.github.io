Call of Cthulhu encompasses H.P. Lovecraft's seminal 1928 short story and Chaosium's tabletop RPG system, alongside a 2018 video game adaptation developed by Cyanide Studio and published by Focus Home Interactive. The narrative centers on Private Investigator Pierce exploring the Hawkins family tragedy on Darkwater Island in 1924 to uncover cosmic horrors and cultist conspiracies linked to the entity Cthulhu. The original text describes the 'monster of vaguely anthropoid outline' with an octopus-like head, while the game utilizes Unreal Engine to simulate sanity loss and investigation mechanics.

Key Findings

  • [Video Game Specifications]: The video game 'Call of Cthulhu' was released on October 29, 2018. It is developed by Cyanide Studio and published by Focus Home Interactive (also listed as Focus Entertainment). The game is built using Unreal Engine and features a protagonist named Private Investigator Pierce who investigates the Hawkins family death on Darkwater Island in 1924. User reviews indicate 'Mostly Positive' status with 76% positive ratings from 4,934 English reviews. (Fonte: Save 85% on Call of Cthulhu® on Steam)
  • [Literary Origin and Content]: The short story 'The Call of Cthulhu' was written by H.P. Lovecraft in the summer of 1926 and first published in the pulp magazine Weird Tales in February 1928. The narrative involves the discovery of a cult worshipping the Great Old One Cthulhu, who is buried in the city of R’lyeh. A key artifact described is a statuette between seven and eight inches high, depicting a monster with an octopus-like head, scaly body, claws, and wings. (Fonte: "The Call of Cthulhu" by H. P. Lovecraft)
  • [Tabletop RPG System]: Chaosium Inc. publishes the 'Call of Cthulhu' role-playing game, which serves as the official inspiration for the video game adaptation. The system includes rules, scenarios (such as 'Down Darker Trails'), and collector editions. It is recognized among the '100 Best Games' and provides resources for investigators dealing with cosmic horror themes. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu)
