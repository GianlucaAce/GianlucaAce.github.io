Sintesi Principale

Recent news for Call of Cthulhu includes the release of 'The Keeper Companion' and 'The Encyclopedia Cthuliana', the announcement of Miskatonic Repository Con 2026, upcoming releases like 'Secrets of the Tome', and community discussions regarding character sheets.

Fatti Chiave

  • [Release of The Keeper Companion]: The Call of Cthulhu: Keeper Companion is a new hardcover book priced at $54.99 (including PDF) or $29.99 for the PDF alone from Chaosium and DriveThruRPG. It contains rules options, three scenarios ('Little Miracles', 'If I Had a Hammer', 'What a Puzzler'), maps, handouts, revised investigator sheets, Pulp Cthulhu hero sheets, new methods for creating investigators, systems for investigator organizations, streamlined rules for skill rolls/combat/chases, sanity rule revisions, and advice on online play and scenario design. (Fonte: Two Mythos Masterpieces out now for Halloween: The Keeper Companion and The Encyclopedia Cthuliana - Chaosium Inc.)
  • [Release of The Encyclopedia Cthuliana]: The Encyclopedia Cthuliana is a fully revised and updated resource for cosmic horror enthusiasts, covering topics from Azathoth to Zylac. It serves as an essential reference for gamers, collectors, and researchers. (Fonte: Two Mythos Masterpieces out now for Halloween: The Keeper Companion and The Encyclopedia Cthuliana - Chaosium Inc.)
  • [Miskatonic Repository Con 2026 Announcement]: The Call of Cthulhu Miskatonic Repository Convention is scheduled for October 23-25, 2026. It is a free three-day virtual convention celebrating user-made content for Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition on the Miskatonic Repository platform. Player sign-ups are live, and GM (Keeper) sign-ups are ongoing via email. (Fonte: Miskatonic Repository Con returns in 2026 (Oct 23-25) for its 7th Year and Player Sign-ups are Live! - Chaosium Inc.)
  • [Upcoming Release: Secrets of the Tome]: A new Call of Cthulhu product titled 'Secrets of the Tome' is scheduled for release on November 1, 2026, from Dark Horse. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu - Enworld)
  • [Community Discussion on Keeper Companion Sheets]: Reddit users are discussing the new character sheets included in The Keeper Companion, noting specific mechanical changes such as the removal of 'Science' as a speciality and adjustments to skill rolls (e.g., preventing accidental diving rolls when asking for driving skills). (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu Keeper's Companion new character sheets : r/callofcthulhu)
