The Horror!

Call of Cthulhu is a tabletop horror RPG by Chaosium Inc., currently in its 7th Edition (released 2014/2016), based on H.P. Lovecraft's works and the Cthulhu Mythos. It utilizes a percentile-based Basic Roleplaying (BRP) system where players assume the role of Investigators facing cosmic horrors, with gameplay centered on investigation rather than combat. Key mechanics include Sanity loss upon encountering the unknown, and the game supports various historical settings beyond its classic 1920s backdrop.

  • Game Mechanics and System: The game uses Chaosium’s Basic Roleplaying (BRP) system, a roll-low percentile (D100) mechanic where success is determined by rolling under the character's skill value. A double zero on the tens die results in a critical failure. The system emphasizes investigation, research, and avoiding direct confrontation, as combat is lethal. Characters are 'Investigators' who risk permanent insanity or death upon encountering cosmic horrors. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition Quick-Start Rules)
  • Edition History and Compatibility: First released in 1981, the game has progressed through multiple editions (1st to 7th). The core percentile system has remained stable, ensuring high compatibility of supplements across versions. The 7th Edition, published by Chaosium Inc., streamlines rules while maintaining backwards compatibility with previous materials. Other adaptations include 'Call of Cthulhu d20' (using D&D 3.5 rules) and specialized settings like 'Cthulhu Invictus' (Ancient Rome) and 'Achtung! Cthulhu' (WWII). (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu - Noble Knight Games)
  • Core Themes and Gameplay Loop: The game focuses on 'cosmic horror' where humanity is insignificant against alien entities. Players investigate mysteries in settings like the 1920s (classic), Victorian era, or modern day. The Game Master is called the 'Keeper of Arcane Lore'. Success is often measured by survival rather than victory, with a focus on creeping dread and the consequences of knowledge. Sanity points measure resilience to mind-bending horror. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu Hub)
  • Literary Origins and Mythos: The RPG is directly adapted from the literary works of H.P. Lovecraft, specifically his 1928 novella 'The Call of Cthulhu'. It draws upon the 'Cthulhu Mythos', a shared fictional universe involving alien god-like entities and arcane lore. The game explicitly avoids Lovecraft’s personal racist views while utilizing the imaginative ideas of the Mythos created by him and contemporaries like Frank Belknap Long and Ramsey Campbell. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition Quick-Start Rules)
