The Horror!

Call of Cthulhu is a horror-themed tabletop roleplaying game (TTRPG) published by Chaosium, based on H.P. Lovecraft's mythos and set primarily in the 1920s. Players assume the roles of ordinary investigators using a roll-low percentile (D100) system to solve mysteries while managing sanity risks. Additionally, an official video game adaptation developed by Cyanide Studio was released in 2018, featuring private investigator Edward Pierce on Darkwater Island.

  • [Tabletop RPG Mechanics and Setting]: Call of Cthulhu is a mystery and horror TTRPG published by Chaosium, typically set in the 1920s (though other eras are available). It uses the Basic Roleplaying engine with a roll-low D100 system where players aim to roll under their skill percentage. The game focuses on investigation rather than combat; characters risk permanent insanity or death upon encountering cosmic horrors, and there is no traditional leveling up of stats. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition Quick-Start Rules)
  • [Video Game Adaptation Details]: An official video game titled 'Call of Cthulhu' was developed by Cyanide Studio and published by Focus Entertainment, released on October 29, 2018. The narrative follows Private Investigator Edward Pierce in 1924 as he investigates the Hawkins family tragedy on Darkwater Island. It is a first-person psychological horror adventure that incorporates sanity mechanics and stealth elements. (Fonte: Save 85% on Call of Cthulhu® on Steam)
  • [Core Gameplay Loop and Roles]: The game involves a Keeper (Game Master) who describes the setting while players control Investigators of the Unknown. The primary goal is to uncover secrets and survive, with victory often defined by escaping or maintaining sanity rather than defeating enemies. Skills improve through use upon success, but characters remain vulnerable to fatal consequences from Mythos entities. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu – Getting Started)
