The Horror!

Call of Cthulhu is a tabletop role-playing game (TTRPG) published by Chaosium, currently in its 7th Edition, based on H.P. Lovecraft's Cthulhu Mythos and set primarily in the 1920s. The game utilizes the Basic Roleplaying (BRP) system, employing a roll-low percentile (D100) mechanic where players assume the roles of 'Investigators' facing cosmic horror and potential madness. A notable digital adaptation was released in 2018 by Cyanide Studio for PC/PlayStation, featuring first-person investigation mechanics.

  • Tabletop RPG Mechanics and Edition: Call of Cthulhu is a horror-themed TTRPG published by Chaosium Inc., currently on its 7th Edition (released in 2018). It uses the Basic Roleplaying (BRP) system, which relies on a roll-low percentile (D100) mechanic: players roll under their skill value to succeed. The game emphasizes investigation over combat, with high lethality; characters risk permanent insanity or death upon exposure to cosmic horrors. The Game Master is referred to as the 'Keeper of Arcane Lore'. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition Quick-Start Rules)
  • Video Game Adaptation Details: An official video game titled 'Call of Cthulhu' was developed by Cyanide Studio and published by Focus Entertainment, released on October 29, 2018. The game is a first-person psychological horror adventure set in 1924, where the player controls Private Investigator Pierce investigating the Hawkins family death on Darkwater Island. It features mechanics for sanity management, stealth, and puzzle-solving within Lovecraft's universe. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu®)
  • Lore and Setting Origins: The game is inspired by H.P. Lovecraft's 1928 novella 'The Call of Cthulhu' and the wider 'Cthulhu Mythos,' a collection of stories sharing common elements like alien gods and arcane lore. The primary setting is the 1920s, though variants exist for other eras (e.g., Ancient Rome in 'Cthulhu Invictus'). The narrative focuses on ordinary people ('Investigators') uncovering secrets that threaten their sanity. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu - Tabletop Role-Playing Games (TTRPGs) - LibGuides at University of Missouri - St Louis)
