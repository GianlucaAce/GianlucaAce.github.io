Call of Cthulhu is primarily a tabletop role-playing game (TTRPG) published by Chaosium, utilizing the Basic Roleplaying system with percentile dice (D100) for skill checks. It features investigators navigating cosmic horror in settings like the 1920s, where sanity mechanics are central to gameplay. Additionally, an official video game adaptation was developed by Cyanide and published by Focus Home Interactive in 2018, set on Darkwater Island.

Key Findings

  • [Tabletop RPG Mechanics and System]: Call of Cthulhu is a horror-themed tabletop role-playing game published by Chaosium Inc. It uses the Basic Roleplaying Universal Game Engine, a skill-based system where players roll percentile dice (D100) to determine success or failure against specific skill ratings. The 7th Edition includes approximately 47 standard skills and emphasizes character development through usage. Sanity is a core mechanic measured in points, affecting how investigators perceive reality as they encounter the Cthulhu Mythos. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition Quick-Start Rules)
  • [Video Game Adaptation Details]: An official video game titled 'Call of Cthulhu' was released on October 30, 2018. It was developed by Cyanide Studio and published by Focus Home Interactive (also listed as Focus Entertainment). The game is set in 1924, following Private Investigator Pierce on Darkwater Island to solve the Hawkins family tragedy. It utilizes the Unreal Engine and features a mature rating for cosmic horror themes. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu - Tabletop Role-Playing Games (TTRPGs) - LibGuides at University of Missouri - St Louis)
  • [Setting and Narrative Premise]: The game is inspired by the fiction of H.P. Lovecraft and the Cthulhu Mythos, typically set in the 1920s (Classic Era) but adaptable to other periods like Victorian or modern times. Players assume the roles of 'investigators'—ordinary people such as professors or detectives—who uncover dark secrets and confront alien forces. The narrative focuses on mystery, survival, and the risk of madness rather than traditional combat victory. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu – Getting Started)
