The Horror!

Call of Cthulhu is a tabletop role-playing game (TTRPG) created by Chaosium, based on H.P. Lovecraft's 1928 novella and the Cthulhu Mythos, where players assume the roles of investigators facing cosmic horror in settings typically ranging from the 1920s to modern times. The game utilizes a skill-based percentile system (d100) under the Basic Roleplaying engine, emphasizing mystery, sanity management, and character development over combat. Additionally, an official video game adaptation was developed by Cyanide and published by Focus Home Interactive in 2018, set in 1924 on Darkwater Island.

  • Tabletop RPG Origins and Mechanics: Call of Cthulhu is a tabletop role-playing game created by Chaosium, inspired by H.P. Lovecraft's fiction and the Cthulhu Mythos. It uses a roll-low D100 percentile system where players attempt to roll under their skill value to succeed. The game focuses on mystery, horror, and character development, with players acting as investigators who risk madness when confronting cosmic entities. The current version is the 7th Edition, which employs Chaosium’s Basic Roleplaying Universal Game Engine. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu Hub)
  • Video Game Adaptation Details: An official video game titled 'Call of Cthulhu' was released on October 29, 2018. It was developed by Cyanide Studio and published by Focus Home Interactive (also listed as Focus Entertainment). The game is set in 1924, following Private Investigator Pierce on Darkwater Island to solve the Hawkins family tragedy. It utilizes the Unreal Engine and features a survival horror gameplay loop involving sanity management and investigation. (Fonte: Save 85% on Call of Cthulhu® on Steam)
  • Literary Source Material: The game is based on the novella 'The Call of Cthulhu' by H.P. Lovecraft, first published in February 1928. This story is significant as it introduces Cthulhu himself, provides the first explicit rationale for the Cthulhu Mythos, and depicts a human seeing a god. The narrative involves a secret cult waiting for the Great Old One Cthulhu to rise from his city of R’lyeh. (Fonte: The Call of Cthulhu by H.P. Lovecraft | Goodreads)
  • Starter Set and Components: The Call of Cthulhu Starter Set includes essential materials for new players: Book 1 (Introduction and 'Alone Against The Flames' solo adventure), Book 2 (Starter Rules), Book 3 (three starter adventures), five ready-to-play investigators, blank investigator sheets, seven polyhedral dice, and player handouts. The game has won over 40 awards and is inducted into the Academy of Adventure Gaming Art and Design Hall of Fame. (Fonte: Chaosium Inc Call of Cthulhu Starter Set)
