The Horror!

Call of Cthulhu is a horror-themed tabletop role-playing game (TTRPG) published by Chaosium, currently in its 7th Edition (released in 2018), which utilizes the Basic Roleplaying (BRP) system with d100 percentile dice mechanics. Inspired by H.P. Lovecraft’s fiction and the Cthulhu Mythos, the game typically casts players as ordinary investigators navigating cosmic horrors and cults, often set in the 1920s, where success is measured by skill rolls and failure risks permanent insanity or death. Additionally, an official video game adaptation developed by Cyanide Studio and published by Focus Entertainment was released on October 29, 2018, allowing players to experience this narrative as a private investigator named Pierce.

  • Tabletop RPG Mechanics and Edition: Call of Cthulhu is a tabletop role-playing game published by Chaosium Inc., currently in its 7th Edition (released in 2018). It uses the Basic Roleplaying (BRP) system, which relies on a roll-low percentile (d100) mechanic where players must roll under their skill percentage to succeed. The game emphasizes investigation over combat, with characters facing lethal consequences such as death or permanent loss of Sanity points when encountering cosmic horrors. (Fonte: Call of Cthulhu 7th Edition Quick-Start Rules)
  • Video Game Adaptation Details: An official video game titled 'Call of Cthulhu' was released on October 29, 2018. Developed by Cyanide Studio and published by Focus Entertainment, it is a first-person psychological horror adventure set in 1924. The player controls Private Investigator Pierce, who investigates the tragic death of the Hawkins family on Darkwater Island, dealing with sanity mechanics, stealth, and puzzle-solving within Lovecraft's universe. (Fonte: Save 85% on Call of Cthulhu® on Steam)
  • Lore and Setting Origins: The game is inspired by the 20th-century horror fiction of H.P. Lovecraft, specifically the short story 'The Call of Cthulhu' (1928), and the broader 'Cthulhu Mythos.' The narrative framework pits ordinary people against alien god-like entities and sinister cults. While traditionally set in the 1920s, the system supports various eras including ancient Rome, the Dark Ages, and modern times.
