What's New in AI?

Recent AI sector developments include Anthropic's historic IPO with a valuation exceeding $2 trillion and shares closing 17% higher on its Nasdaq debut. Concurrently, hyperscalers (Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Oracle) project over $800 billion in AI infrastructure spending for the year, marking a 94% increase from 2025. Meanwhile, French startup Sesterce unveiled a €10 billion data center project in Finland, and Mistral AI launched its 'Mistral Large 4' model to compete with Chinese rivals.

  • Anthropic IPO and Valuation: Anthropic became the first leading AI model developer to list on Wall Street, pricing shares at $83. The company raised funds for long-term commitments including computing power and data centers. Following its debut, shares closed 17% higher, with market valuations topping $2 trillion (initially priced at $1.5 trillion). (Fonte: Artificial Intelligence - World news, culture and opinion)
  • Hyperscaler AI Infrastructure Spending: The five largest U.S. hyperscalers—Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and Oracle—estimate they will spend $800 billion or more on data centers, chips, and other AI-related infrastructure this year. This represents a 94 percent increase over 2025 spending levels. (Fonte: Boom or Bubble? Understanding AI and the U.S. Economy | Council on Foreign Relations)
  • Sesterce Data Center Project: French AI startup Sesterce unveiled a €10 billion project to build a data center in Jamsa, Finland, on the site of a former paper mill. The facility aims to develop supercomputing and computing infrastructure specifically for artificial intelligence. (Fonte: Artificial Intelligence - World news, culture and opinion)
  • Mistral AI Model Release: French startup Mistral AI unveiled its new model, 'Mistral Large 4,' aimed at narrowing the performance gap with top Chinese competitors. The company had been accused of falling behind in AI model performance prior to this release. (Fonte: Artificial Intelligence - World news, culture and opinion)
