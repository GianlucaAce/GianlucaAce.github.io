What's New in AI?

Recent developments highlight Anthropic's release of Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, featuring agentic capabilities demonstrated by 1,000 subagents building an OS in eight days, alongside Google's expansion of Gemini 4 Carbon for complex coding tasks with specific performance benchmarks. Concurrently, OpenAI restricted GPT-6 Cyber access following high boundary adherence evaluations, while broader industry reports emphasize the 'scaling era' and emerging challenges in AI guardrails and on-device memory optimization.

  • Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 with Agentic Demonstrations: Anthropic introduced two models based on the same foundation: Claude Fable 5.2 for broader technical tasks and Claude Mythos 5.2 restricted to verified cybersecurity and life sciences organizations. A key demonstration involved over 1,000 AI subagents collaborating for eight days to build an operating system from scratch, which successfully booted on a laptop with drivers, storage, windows, and a login screen. Anthropic also published research on its 'dreaming' process for memory organization and added physicist Christopher Stubbs to its Long-Term Benefit Trust. (Fonte: AI Insights: Key Global Developments in November 2026)
  • Google Expands Access to Gemini 4 Carbon for Complex Coding: Google expanded access to Gemini 4 Carbon, a model designed for complex coding, debugging, security analysis, and scientific research. Unlike standard tools, Carbon can inspect code, make changes, run tests, and iterate until completion. It integrates with the Antigravity platform for multi-agent coordination. Performance metrics include a score of 59 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 and 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro, with a listed cost of €7.16 per million tokens. (Fonte: AI Insights: Key Global Developments in November 2026)
  • OpenAI Restricts GPT-6 Cyber Access Based on Evaluation Results: OpenAI restricted access to GPT-6 Cyber to authorized cybersecurity researchers. This decision followed evaluations showing 99.93% out-of-scope boundary adherence without deployment-specific instructions and 100% adherence with them, mirroring Anthropic's limitation of Claude Mythos 5.2 to verified organizations. (Fonte: AI Insights: Key Global Developments in November 2026)
  • Industry Report Defines 'Scaling Era' and Highlights AI Guardrail Vulnerabilities: The AI100 report 'AI Goes Mainstream' identifies the last five years as a 'scaling era,' noting ChatGPT's unprecedented growth of 100 million users within two months. Concurrently, new research indicates that AI guardrails are easier to break than assumed, with tests showing repeated harassment in simulated conversations despite heavy investment in refusal training. Additionally, innovations include shorter AI training notes to reduce memory demands on small devices. (Fonte: AI Goes Mainstream: The Scaling Era and the Rise of Generative AI)
