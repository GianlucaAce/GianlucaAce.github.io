Wizards of the Coast faces internal labor unrest as D&D and MTG teams announce unionization plans with the CWA to address layoffs and AI concerns. Concurrently, the publisher confirms two major 'Universes Beyond' collaborations: a Star Wars expansion releasing in 2027 featuring Tyro Squadron adventures, and a World of Warcraft crossover arriving November 17, 2026.

Key Findings

  • [Unionization Effort at Wizards of the Coast]: Designers, artists, writers, and workers behind Dungeons & Dragons, Magic: The Gathering, and D&D Beyond have informed leadership of their intent to unionize with the Communications Workers of America. They demand layoff protections, generative AI safeguards, remote work policies, fiscal transparency, and sustainable workloads, citing recent layoffs on the MTG and D&D teams as a catalyst. (Fonte: Big Dungeons & Dragons news as D&D inform Wizards of the Coast of their plan to unionize)
  • [Star Wars Collaboration Details]: Wizards of the Coast announced two upcoming Star Wars sourcebooks under the Universes Beyond imprint, set for release in 2027. The first is 'Tyro Squadron: Adventures,' focusing on Rebel crews during the Original Trilogy era. Specific mechanical changes for blasters and lightsabers are pending further disclosure. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons Officially Reveals First Details on Star Wars Collaboration)
  • [World of Warcraft Crossover Release Date]: The D&D: World of Warcraft expansion, allowing players to explore familiar zones and dungeons from the franchise's history, is scheduled for release on November 17. This includes a core sourcebook and an Icecrown Citadel Map Pack with poster-sized maps, tokens, and a digital adventure.
