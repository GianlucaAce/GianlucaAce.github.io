Sintesi Principale

Recent D&D news includes the launch of new subclasses and feats on D&D Beyond (Sorrow Knight, Shadow Pact), significant organizational changes at Wizards of the Coast (new studio in Montreal, unionization efforts by D&D and Studio X teams), and upcoming releases such as a Dark Sun book for 2027, a World of Warcraft world book, and a classic dungeon map book. Additionally, Hasbro opened a new studio focused on D&D content.

Fatti Chiave

  • [D&D Beyond October Drop: Sorrow Knight and Shadow Pact]: D&D Beyond released its October drop featuring the first-ever subclass for Fighters, the 'Sorrow Knight,' which connects to the Shadowfell. It includes features like sensing prevalent emotions at level 3 and attuning to the Shadowfell after using Action Surge at level 18. The drop also introduced the 'Shadow Pact' feat, a player-facing option complementing the Fey Pact, along with new Planar Pact feats and General feats. (Fonte: D&D Beyond’s October Drop: Shadowfell Secrets! | Dungeons & Dragons)
  • [Unionization of D&D and Studio X Teams]: On October 6, 2026, the Dungeons & Dragons team (including workers behind D&D and D&D Beyond) and Studio X (covering Magic: The Gathering and Duel Masters teams) announced their intent to unionize with the Communications Workers of America (CWA). They called on Wizards of the Coast leadership to voluntarily recognize the union by October 13th, citing a need for respect, dignity, and fair treatment as stewards of the company's profitable franchises. (Fonte: Dungeons & Dragons and Studio X Join United Wizards of the Coast - CWA | United Wizards of the Coast)
  • [Hasbro Opens New D&D Focused Studio in Montreal]: Hasbro has opened a new Wizards of the Coast studio located in Montréal. This facility is specifically focused on developing content for the Dungeons & Dragons franchise. (Fonte: Hasbro opens studio focused on Dungeons & Dragons)
  • [Upcoming D&D Releases: Dark Sun, World of Warcraft, and Map Book]: Several upcoming releases were announced: 'Dark Sun' is scheduled to return in 2027; a 'Dungeons & Dragons: World of Warcraft' world book for Azeroth is set for November 3, 2026; and a walkthrough map book titled 'Dungeons! Illustrated Maps Of Classic Dungeons & Dragons Adventures' will be available on April 27, 2027, in hardcover for $30. (Fonte: News Digest for October 24, 2026 | EN World D&D & Tabletop RPG News & Reviews)
  • [DDO Update 82: The Court of Strahd Event]: Dungeons & Dragons Online (DDO) released Update 82, introducing 'The Court of Strahd' event. Strahd returns with a new ally, and the Order of the Guardians requires assistance. The event runs from October 14th until February 17th, 2027. The update also adjusted mount appearances for Night Revels dungeons and allowed the Resurrection Chamber to be played up to Level 41. (Fonte: Update 82 Release Notes | Dungeons & Dragons Online)
  • [D&D Design Team Expansion with Celeste Conowitch]: Veteran tabletop RPG designer Celeste Conowitch has left Kobold Press and joined the official D&D design team at Wizards of the Coast, contributing to the growth of the design staff. (Fonte: D&D Weekly News Roundup (October 5th, 2026))
