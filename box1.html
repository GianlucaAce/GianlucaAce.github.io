Wizards of the Coast has officially rebranded the 2024 update as 'Dungeons & Dragons 5.5e' and is rebuilding D&D Beyond for a 2027 digital shift. Key releases include the 'Dungeons & Dragons: World of Warcraft' crossover book (Nov 2026), new Ravenloft campaigns, and the 'Dungeon Masters' actual play series starring Jasmine Bhullar. Upcoming titles feature Dark Sun (2027), Greyhawk, and Star Wars expansions.

Key Findings

  • [Official Rebranding to D&D 5.5e]: Wizards of the Coast has officially named the 2024 rules update 'Dungeons & Dragons 5.5e'. This rebranding applies across all platforms, including D&D Beyond, marking a shift from previous ambiguous naming conventions for the edition. (Fonte: Hasbro Just Changed Dungeons & Dragons Forever)
  • [D&D Beyond Digital Roadmap and Rebuild]: Chris Cox confirmed that D&D Beyond is being rebuilt in 2026 for a major shakeup in 2027, moving towards a 'digital first' strategy. This includes new features like the Maps VTT improvements, accessibility updates (Disability Pride Dice integration), and a forced migration to their digital ecosystem. (Fonte: D&D Beyond | Play Your Way With the Official D&D Toolset)
  • [Dungeons & Dragons: World of Warcraft Crossover]: A new world book titled 'Dungeons & Dragons: World of Warcraft' is scheduled for release on November 3, 2026. This collaboration allows players to bring Azeroth into the D&D system, with a physical and digital bundle available for preorder. (Fonte: EN World: D&D & Tabletop RPG News & Reviews)
  • [Dungeon Masters Actual Play Series]: Wizards of the Coast announced 'Dungeon Masters,' an official actual play series debuting April 22, 2026, on YouTube. The series features Jasmine Bhullar as the Dungeon Master and stars Mayanna Berrin, Christian Navarro, Neil Newbon, and Devora Wilde, showcasing unreleased D&D content. (Fonte: Wizards of the Coast Announces Dungeon Masters)
  • [Upcoming 2027 Releases: Dark Sun, Greyhawk, and Star Wars]: The official release schedule includes 'Dark Sun' returning in 2027, 'Greyhawk: Crown of the Witch Queen' (160-page adventure) on July 31, 2027, and 'Star Wars: Season of Rebellion' as an official D&D book on June 15, 2027. (Fonte: EN World: D&D & Tabletop RPG News & Reviews)
