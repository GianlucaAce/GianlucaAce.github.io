Wizards of the Coast is advancing D&D 5.5E with new physical monster and spell cards for CR 0-5 encounters and magical options from the 2024 Player’s Handbook, alongside an Unearthed Arcana playtest titled 'Villainous Options Update' featuring eight new feats and three revised subclasses. Dan Ayoub has been officially named head of Wizards of the Coast, while D&D Beyond is rolling out updates for smoother onboarding and shared dice features in 2026. Additionally, major collaborations include a Star Wars-themed expansion arriving in 2027, a Dark Sun graphic novel trilogy by Dark Horse Comics, and VR content via the 'Demeo x D&D: Battlemarked' Acquisitions Incorporated update.
