Recent AI sector developments are dominated by a sharp correction in tech and semiconductor stocks following US midterm election concerns and physical infrastructure constraints. While hyperscalers like Microsoft reported significant capex shortfalls due to data center delays, Anthropic achieved its first operating profit ahead of its IPO filing.
